About Us

Collaboration Beyond Company Boundaries

Alliance to Zero is a non-profit membership association for pharma and biotech supply chain companies. We were founded on 2 June 2021 in Burgdorf, Switzerland by eight companies representing key parts of the pharmaceutical supply chain.

While there is much discussion in the pharma community about sustainability and climate impact, substantial change among supply chain partners and manufacturers is not happening fast enough to reduce the emissions footprint. This is what the Alliance to Zero is committed to changing.

Our member companies represent component suppliers, machine suppliers, assembly and manufacturing service providers, as well as those involved in primary and secondary packaging through to final device assembly and collection after use.

Roadmap

Our Ambitions

A phased journey toward net zero pharmaceutical manufacturing and supply chain.

2023 Achieved

Awareness & Network

Raised awareness and created a strong industry network. Specific programs to reduce GHG emissions — including waste reduction in injection devices — established within member companies. The full supply chain of injection devices is now represented by the Alliance.

2026 In Progress

Business Model Innovation

Business models collaboratively developed based on GHG emissions reduction and Circular Economy principles. Multiple supply chain solutions to significantly decarbonise injection devices, usable across a wide variety of existing and future devices.

2030 Target

Net Zero Launch

Member company operations covering the supply chain of the net zero product offering will be net zero or show significant GHG emissions reduction. Alliance solutions will have contributed to the launch of the first generation of net zero injection devices.
